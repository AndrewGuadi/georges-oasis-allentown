Family parties & celebrations

A birthday, a shower, or finally getting everyone in the same room.

Maybe it’s your mom’s birthday and relatives are driving in from three directions. Maybe you’re hosting a shower and need room for gifts, food, and a few people who’ll show up early to help. Or maybe you just want the whole family together somewhere everyone can sit and talk.

Start with an approximate headcount. We’ll help you work out where the group can sit, how to handle food, and what else you’d like to include.

A family meal together
Ask about seating your group together and having guests choose from the restaurant menu.
A celebration in the back room
Ask about the room for your date and tell us if you need space for gifts, decorations, or cake.
A birthday with something to do
During golf season, ask about starting with a round and getting together afterward for food or ice cream.

Office & team lunches

A freshly made lunch, with time to get back to work.

You have a lunch break, a group to feed, and a time everyone needs to be back. Come to the Oasis for char-grilled burgers, wraps, salads, and rice bowls in a spacious dining room where the team can hear each other talk.

Tell us how many are coming, when you can arrive, and when the last person needs to leave. We’ll confirm what we can accommodate before you put it on everyone’s calendar.

A lunch break with a hard stop
Give us your arrival and departure times so we can talk through menu choices and timing.
A lunch the company is paying for
Share your headcount, budget per person, and whether you need one check.
A monthly team lunch
Tell us the day and time that usually work for your team and how often you’d like to come.

Mini golf & group outings

Play a round. Grab a bite. Finish with a scoop.

Follow the course past the waterfall and see who gets bragging rights. Then head over for a bite or let the kids pick their ice cream. The course and restaurant are at the same location.

Coming with the family? Check the rates and season before you head over. Children age 3 and under play free. For a school trip, youth group, or fundraiser, tell us how many are coming and what you’d like to include.

Golf and a scoop
Play a round, then meet up for ice cream. A simple format for a birthday or family group.
Golf and lunch
Play first and sit down to eat afterward, or ask about lunch before you head out to the course.
A school trip or fundraiser
Share your number of students and adults, your arrival and departure times, and whether you need to keep groups together.
