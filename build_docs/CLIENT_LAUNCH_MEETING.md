# George’s Oasis client launch review

A 30-minute working meeting to decide whether the proposed website can launch, can launch with agreed omissions, or needs to wait. Record approval of the exact version shown; a general “looks good” does not approve prices, hours, or operational readiness.

## Prepare before the meeting

- Open the current public preview on a desktop and phone. Have the ten public pages ready in tabs, plus the owner inbox and private menu preview. Keep draft content clearly identified.
- Bring the latest technical check results and [existing owner review](OWNER_REVIEW.md). Previous checks passed, but live hosting, provider transactions and notification delivery still require verification for launch.
- Ask the owner to bring the current menu, current service hours, and the name of the person responsible for inquiries. Missing materials become assigned follow-up work.
- Prepare a clearly labeled test inquiry with staff awareness. Do not place a real order, charge a card, or reserve a table simply to demonstrate a button.
- Have the proposed hosting/domain arrangement ready. Discuss the responsible account holder; exchange passwords and other credentials separately through a secure method.

## Keep the meeting to 30 minutes

For each topic, record **approved**, **change before launch**, or **defer with an agreed public fallback**. If an answer takes more than a minute to establish, assign it in the worksheet and move on. Park cosmetic preferences unless they make the site confusing or unusable.

## Questions to use during the walkthrough

Ask the owner to describe what they expect to happen before clicking:

- “You’re a first-time guest on your phone. How would you decide whether to come today?”
- “You want dinner to go. Where would you click, and what would you expect next?”
- “You’re planning a birthday. After sending this form, would you think your date was reserved?”
- “A request comes in tomorrow and the email notice doesn’t arrive. Who still sees it?”

A mismatch between the expected and actual result is a concrete correction. Record the page and the desired behavior rather than a vague request to “make it clearer.”

## Launch decision gates

Mark each row **Pass**, **Open**, or **Accepted omission**. An omission passes only after the owner approves the alternative, the team implements it, and it is verified. A name and deadline alone do not close a gate.
